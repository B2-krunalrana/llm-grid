1. Introduction

LLM Grid (“the Application”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is an open-source software project developed by Krunal Rana. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use LLM Grid — whether through a self-hosted deployment, a hosted service we may offer, or any other interaction with the Application.

We are committed to transparency and data minimization. We do not sell your personal data, your prompts, or your API credentials. By using LLM Grid, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

Key Principle LLM Grid is a conduit — it connects you to third-party AI providers. Your prompts pass through the Application to the provider(s) you select. We store as little as possible on our own infrastructure.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate, improve, and secure the Application. This falls into the following categories:

Account Data
If a hosted version requires registration, we may collect your name, email address, and a hashed password. Self-hosted instances collect no account data on our servers.
Usage Analytics
Basic, anonymized metrics such as page views, feature usage frequency, and aggregated request counts. We use privacy-respecting analytics where possible.
Error Logs
Diagnostic logs including stack traces, timestamps, and anonymized request identifiers to help us debug crashes and improve stability.
Configuration Metadata
Non-sensitive settings such as selected provider names, model preferences, and theme choices — stored locally or on our hosted servers.

We do NOT collect: the content of your prompts, the AI-generated responses, or your API keys on our central servers (see Section 3).

3. API Keys and Credentials

LLM Grid allows you to connect your own third-party API keys (e.g., from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.). Here is how we handle them:

  • Self-Hosted Deployments: API keys are stored exclusively on your own infrastructure — in environment variables, a local database, or secure vault of your choosing. We have no access to them.
  • Hosted Version (if applicable): Keys are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and transmitted only over HTTPS. They are stored in a segregated credential store and are never logged or exposed in plaintext.
  • Transmission: Keys are forwarded solely to the AI provider(s) you explicitly select for each request. They are never sent to any other third party.
Your Responsibility You are responsible for the security of your own API keys. We recommend rotating keys regularly and using provider-side usage limits. LLM Grid is not liable for unauthorized charges resulting from compromised keys.

4. How Information Is Used

We use the limited information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  1. Service Operation — to route your requests to the correct AI provider and return responses.
  2. Performance & Reliability — to monitor uptime, detect anomalies, and optimize response latency.
  3. Bug Fixing — to diagnose and resolve software errors through error logs and crash reports.
  4. Product Improvement — aggregated, anonymized analytics help us understand which features are most valuable.
  5. Communication — if you contact us via email, we use your email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

5. Third-Party AI Providers

LLM Grid integrates with multiple AI/LLM providers and aggregators. When you submit a prompt, it is transmitted directly to the provider(s) you have configured. Each provider has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. We strongly encourage you to review them:

  • OpenAI — Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — Privacy Policy
  • Google (Gemini) — Privacy Policy
  • Other providers as configured by you.

LLM Grid does not control how these providers process, store, or use your prompts. Some providers may retain prompts for training or safety purposes depending on their own policies and your agreement with them.

6. Data Retention

  • Error Logs: Retained for up to 30 days, then automatically purged.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized; retained indefinitely in aggregate form. Individual event-level data is discarded after 90 days.
  • Account Data (hosted version): Retained until you delete your account or request removal. Deletion is processed within 14 business days.
  • API Keys (hosted version): Immediately deleted upon account termination or key removal from the interface.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information:

  • All communications use TLS 1.3 (HTTPS) encryption in transit.
  • Sensitive credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM.
  • We follow the principle of least privilege for all internal access.
  • Regular dependency audits and vulnerability scanning are part of our CI/CD pipeline.
  • The open-source codebase allows independent security review by the community.

However, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting hosted users, we will notify affected parties within 72 hours of discovery.

8. Cookies and Analytics

The hosted version of LLM Grid may use minimal cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Session tokens for authentication (if applicable). These are strictly necessary and do not track you across sites.
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-focused, self-hosted analytics (e.g., Plausible or Umami) that do not use cookies and are GDPR-compliant out of the box. No personal data is collected via analytics.

The self-hosted version does not include any tracking mechanisms by default. You control your own deployment.
